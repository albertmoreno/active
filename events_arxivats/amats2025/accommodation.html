Accommodation

Accommodation for AMatS 2025 participants near ALBA Synchrotron

A block of 20 rooms has been reserved at the student residence located next to the ALBA Synchrotron for AMatS 2025 participants.
Reservations are available under the same conditions as for ALBA users.

Details:

  • Dates: Check-in on 24 November 2025, check-out on 26 November 2025
  • Rooms: 20 rooms blocked until 6 November 2025
  • Reference code: AMATS-ALBA (please mention this code when making your reservation)
  • Payment: Each guest is responsible for their own payment upon booking
  • Rate: €68 + 10% VAT per room per night, breakfast included

Address:
Student Residence – ALBA Area
Avinguda de la Ciència, 08290 Cerdanyola del Vallès (Barcelona) [Located just a 5-minute walk from the ALBA Synchrotron.]

For your stay during the conference, you have two main options depending on your preferences:

Stay on campus for convenience: If you want to be close to the conference venue and avoid commuting, we recommend booking a room at Hotel EXE Campus, located within the UAB campus. This is the most convenient option, allowing you to be among the first to arrive at the sessions without worrying about transportation and you don’t mind walking 15-20 minutes.

Stay in Barcelona for a lively experience: If you prefer to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of Barcelona after the conference, we suggest booking accommodation near the Gràcia neighborhood (Gràcia, Provença, or Pl. Molina). Gràcia is known for its cosmopolitan and bohemian ambiance, with a wide range of restaurants, cafes, and cultural spots. The Gràcia FGC train stations is directly connected to the UAB campus, making the commute approximately 30 minutes. Another option would be around the Provença FGC train station or the Plaça Catalunya FGC train station.

Explore Accommodation Options

You can also explore accommodations through the following platforms:

  • Booking.com – Wide selection of hotels and apartments.
  • Airbnb.com – A great option if you prefer staying in private homes.
  • Hotels.com – Another platform offering hotel bookings.

We recommend booking your stay early, as Barcelona is a popular destination and accommodations fill up quickly.
