AMatS2025
4th Conference on Advanced Materials in Spain

24th - 26th November 2025
Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) Campus

Download the Conference Program and Abstracts here

Welcome to the 4th Conference on Advanced Materials in Spain (AMatS 2025) that will take place from November 24th to 26th November 2025 in the Campus of Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB), Cerdanyola del Vallès, Barcelona.

The fourth edition aims to be a discussion forum on the current status and perspectives of the different types of advanced materials. We will provide once again a high-level platform where recognized experts and participants will share new developments, research and testing technologies that will surely contribute and inspire you in to moving advanced materials one step further!

As in the previous edition in Valladolid, the conference will not only be attended by groups involved in the Complementary Program of Advanced Materials but also extends its invitation to any materials science enthusiast in general, with interest in exploring the most recent advances and sharing their knowledge and experience.

The conference will be organized into 4 thematic sessions composed of invited talks as well as contributed scientific communications in three modalities: oral, flash communication, and poster.

The meeting will start on November 24th at 14:30 AM and finish on November 26th at 6:00 PM. More information about the schedule will be provided later in the program.

We look forward to seeing you in the UAB Campus in November 2025!

Sincerely,

The Organizing Committee
