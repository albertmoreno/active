3rd Scientific Meeting of BNC-b Students
November 7-8th, 2017

Welcome

After the successful previous editions of the "1st Meeting of ICMAB Students" in 2011, "1st Meeting of ICMAB-ICN-CIN2 Students" in 2013 and "1st and 2nd Scientific Meeting of BNC-b students" in 2015 and 2016, we now present the "3rd Scientific Meeting of BNC-b Students". This 2017 edition is aimed to present the work performed by the PhD students of the whole Barcelona Nanotechnology Cluster – Bellaterra (ICMAB, ICN2, IMB-CNM, UAB, ALBA), promoting the networking between them in an academic and scientific environment. The event incorporates all the framework of the UAB Research Park, as well as other universities as UB or UPC. Moreover, it is also open to master and undergraduate students, and the scientific community at large. The meeting structure will include the usual oral presentations and posters. Furthermore, we will gather different invited speakers to share with us their experience working in companies, as magazine editors or in the world of divulgation. They will also talk about the skills acquired during the PhD that can be useful outside the academic world, so this event represents a good chance to meet people who have already followed this path and who can give you good advice. Here you will have the opportunity to meet other PhD students, and share your experience and research from different nanoscience areas.

Programme

Topics

The program is organized into different topics, all related to the "nano universe", allowing you to focus on your specialty as well as expand your knowledge in other areas. Attendees will be able to learn from over 50 different researches, including oral sessions, poster presentations, invited speakers and a round table, and also have fun with some monologues related with the scientific world.

Subject areas

  • Nanodevices
  • Nanomaterials
  • Energy and Environment
  • Theory and simulation
  • Synthesis and characterization
  • Nanofabrication
  • Bioapplications
  • Other

Format

The speakers will have 10 minutes for the presentation and 5 minutes to answer possible questions. The talks will be in English. Don't worry if you don't have a good level, the best place to practice is this friendly environment.
