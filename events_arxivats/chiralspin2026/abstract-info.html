Abstract Info

Abstracts should be submitted upon registration. Only one abstract per participant is permitted.

All scientifically sound abstracts will be accepted for poster presentation on Monday, 26 January 2026 (Day 1 of the school).

The best abstract (based on scientific merit) will be selected for a contributed talk (15 minutes: 12 min talk + 3 min Q&A) and communicated to the presenter on 21 January 2026.

A Best Poster Award (selected by a scientific committee) will be given at the end of the School.
